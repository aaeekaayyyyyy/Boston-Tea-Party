26 U.S. Code § 1 - Tax imposed

Subtitle A — Income Taxes. Chapter 1 — Normal Taxes. Subchapter A — Determination of Tax Liability. Part I — Tax Imposed.

(a) Married individuals filing joint returns and surviving spouses

There is hereby imposed on the taxable income of— (1) every married individual (as defined in section 7703) who makes a single return jointly with his spouse under section 6013, and (2) every surviving spouse (as defined in section 2(a)), a tax determined in accordance with the following table.

If taxable income is: Not over $36,900 — The tax is: 15% of taxable income. Over $36,900 but not over $89,150 — $5,535, plus 28% of the excess over $36,900.
